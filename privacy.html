Privacy

What this site keeps, and what it sends where

Last updated: 6 October 2026

In short

  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking or analytics, no cookies set by this site. (The live ship map from VesselFinder, shown for sea exits, follows its own cookie rules.)
  • Your Daily Dig scores stay in your own browser. We never receive them.
  • To show maps, street names, satellite photos and descriptions, your browser contacts a few outside services, listed below.
  • We have no server and no database, so we hold no personal data about you.

Who runs this site

Dig Straight Through is a free, personal, non-commercial project by Arman. For any question about privacy, write to digstraightthrough@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

If you play the Daily Dig, your browser saves your results so you can see your streak and history: the day number, your score, how far off your guess was, and the point you guessed. This is stored with your browser's own storage (called "local storage"), only on that device and in that browser. It is never sent to us or anyone else.

To delete it, clear this site's data in your browser settings. Your history and streak will then start again from zero.

What your browser sends to other services

Like any website that shows maps, this site asks other services for map images and information. When your browser does that, the service receives your internet (IP) address and basic browser information, plus what is listed below. Each service handles that under its own privacy policy.

Your location

The site only asks for your location if you press Use my location, and your browser will ask for your permission first. Your position is used inside the page to set the starting point. Like any starting point, it is then sent to Esri to find the street name, and the map around it is loaded from OpenStreetMap, as described above. We never store it.

Sharing

The share picture is made inside your browser; nothing is uploaded by us. A shared link contains your starting point, rounded to about 100 metres, so it never points to an exact house. Names shown for a starting point leave out house numbers for the same reason. If you share through WhatsApp, X or your phone's share menu, that service's own privacy rules apply.

Links to other websites

Some buttons open other websites in a new tab, for example ship-tracking sites (MarineTraffic, VesselFinder), OpenStreetMap or Wikipedia. Nothing is sent to them unless you click. Once you are on their site, their own privacy policy and cookie rules apply.

What we don't do

  • No user accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
  • No selling or sharing of data. We don't collect any to begin with.
  • Fonts and code libraries are served from this site itself, so your browser does not contact Google or code-hosting services when you visit.

Your rights

Because this site stores no personal data on any server, there is nothing about you for us to show, correct or delete. Your Daily Dig scores are under your control in your own browser. For the outside services above, you can contact them through their privacy policies. You can always write to us at digstraightthrough@gmail.com, and you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.
